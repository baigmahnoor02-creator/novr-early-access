Intelligence you wear,
not one more screen.

For years the smartest thing you owned pulled your eyes downward. NOVR CORE 1 is the opposite. A calm, wearable assistant designed to listen, to see what you see, and to answer hands-free, so your attention stays on the world in front of you.

What it does

Built around presence.

Voice In development

Built so asking feels natural, with no menus and no friction. Just speak the way you'd speak to a person.

“Summarize this label.”

Vision In development

“What am I looking at?”

Hands-free

Worn, not held. Nothing to pull out, unlock, or stare into. Your hands and eyes stay free.

Presence

Designed to answer when you ask and stay silent when you don't. Technology that gives your attention back.
